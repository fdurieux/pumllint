SWOT of the proposed approach

Strengths

  • What exists at each layer is recoverable, and an independent rebuild measures how much the layers together carry.
  • Automated checks are cheap and decisive on structure: missing tests, gaps in decision tables, drift between diagrams.
  • Every item carries its source, its date and who validated it. Everything runs on premises.

Weaknesses

  • Each layer gives only what it holds, never why. Judgement, history and the network need people.
  • Documents derived from the code inherit its defects. A test calibrated on the same code hides them.
  • The evidence so far is one program, one domain and single runs.

Opportunities

  • Departures and reorganisations are scheduled, so a pilot has a natural case.
  • Audit and regulators reward provenance and a stated remainder.
  • Rules that live only in memory become a backlog for automation.

Threats

  • False confidence: a corpus that answers most questions and reads as complete.
  • The single point of failure moves from the expert to the corpus and its owner.
  • The AI layer serves judgement as advice, in a regulated domain.
