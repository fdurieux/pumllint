The pilot: one departing expert, eight weeks before and twelve after

Week 1

Mandate and scope. Name the owner, the facilitator, the engineer and the successor.

Weeks 1 to 2

Inventory from traces: tickets, documents, questions asked. Reverse engineering of the systems in scope.

Weeks 3 to 6

Sessions with the successor present: worked cases, exceptions, incidents, quirks, decisions, the network.

Weeks 4 to 7

The corpus and the AI question layer are built as the sessions run.

Weeks 7 to 8

The grading test: the real question set, graded by the expert, run twice.

Week 8

Freeze the corpus as of the last day. Hand over with the remainder list and a sign-off.

Weeks +1 to +12

The successor works from the corpus. Every question is logged as answered, answered elsewhere, or lost. A drift report closes the pilot.

Done when: every high-consequence item is validated and in its home or named in the remainder. The grading test has run twice. The corpus is frozen with an owner. The successor is in role.

Then: the compliance-critical and high-dependency systems, with the kit and its checks in the delivery pipeline. Indicative effort per case: 30 to 40 expert hours, 15 to 20 facilitator days, 10 to 15 engineer days.

Source: the departing-expert plan, pumllint repository, 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-27
