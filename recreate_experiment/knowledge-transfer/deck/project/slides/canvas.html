Opportunity canvas

1. What problem do we want to solve

Knowledge about our systems is held by a few people and in code nobody reads fast. When they leave or are busy, the reasons behind rules and workarounds are lost. The result is operational risk, slow onboarding and audit exposure.

2. Users

The successor and the new joiner. The expert as source. The product owner and risk as accountable. Audit as reader.

3. Solutions today

Ad-hoc questions to the expert. Handover notes written in the last weeks. Wiki pages that drift from the code.

4. How we go about it

Observe what exists at every layer, from process models to code, and run the check for each. Let an on-premises AI draft rules and explanations, marked as drafts. The expert confirms, rejects or explains each item. Accepted items go to their proper home with a check. A test independent of the code runs beside the documentation. An inventory of what the expert alone knows comes first.

5. How it is used

The successor asks the AI layer, which cites a source or abstains. Engineers read the kit beside the code. Audit reads the register.

6. Adoption

One departing expert as the pilot. Then the compliance-critical and high-dependency systems.

7. User metrics

Successor questions answered from the corpus, elsewhere, or lost. Requests to the expert’s former role.

8. Business problem

Key-person risk. Continuity through turnover. Audit-ready evidence of what the systems do and why.

9. Budget

Per case: 30 to 40 expert hours, 15 to 20 facilitator days, 10 to 15 engineer days. Tooling on premises.

10. Business metrics

Items found, validated, written down and checked, with the remainder named. Rebuild results. Audit findings.

Format: Jeff Patton’s opportunity canvas

8

Patton’s ten boxes. The two dark boxes are the ones that matter most to this audience: the problem we want to solve and how we go about it. The other eight are filled with draft content to be adjusted with the product owner. The budget figures are indicative estimates from the departing-expert plan and are to be replaced by measured figures after the pilot.
