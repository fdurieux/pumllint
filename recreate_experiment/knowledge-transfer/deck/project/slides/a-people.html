The people side: spend the expert’s hours on what nobody else knows

Inventory first

Mine the expert’s work before any interview: tickets resolved, requirements and tests written, questions colleagues asked, incident reports.

Rate each item by consequence if lost, by uniqueness, and by where it is enforced: system, control or memory.

Sessions

Ninety minutes, one theme each, the successor present from the first.

  • A worked case, end to end
  • A counterexample that flips the outcome
  • An exception walk
  • An incident replay
  • Quirks and orphans adjudicated
  • Decision history and the network map

The AI question layer

It cites a source or it abstains. Every answer is labelled: system behaviour, recorded decision, or who to ask.

A judgement item is never applied as a rule. It is shown as a worked case with its author and date.

Every question is logged. Credit-related answers are auditable. The layer assists. A person decides.

Source: the departing-expert plan, pumllint repository

A2

The failure mode the inventory prevents is spending the expert’s hours on what is already written or on what three colleagues also know. The question layer’s answer policy is what keeps it from becoming an adviser in a regulated domain.
