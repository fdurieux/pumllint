Executive summary

The risk

Critical knowledge sits with a few experts and in code nobody reads fast. When they leave, the reasons behind rules and workarounds go with them.

The claim

Each layer of the lifecycle, from business rules to code and operations, holds its own knowledge. What exists can be recovered and checked, layer by layer.

The limit

Each layer gives only what it holds, never why. A gap upstream flows into everything derived from it. A defect in the code flows back into its documents.

The approach

Observe every layer and run its check. Let AI draft explanations and mark them as drafts. Have the expert confirm or reject each one. Keep a check that does not depend on the layer it validates.

The measure

No percentage of knowledge captured. A counted inventory, a named remainder, and two rebuild tests: one for the system and one for the successor.
