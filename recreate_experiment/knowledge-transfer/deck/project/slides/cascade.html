The reduced view of the systemic impact model, version 0.3, drawn from its JSON. Read it left to right. Knowledge held only by people feeds unresolved upstream intent and the assumptions a builder has to make. Those flow into the derived artefacts, through the layer the builder trusts most, into the code, and out into use. The code also writes back into the artefacts derived from it, and a dependency change can alter behaviour with no intent behind it. Four levers sit where the capture practices act: elicit what only people know, have the owner validate every claim, externalise into the normative home with a register that drift can be measured against, and keep an oracle calibrated independently of the code. Solid lines carry a bounded measurement from the recreation experiment, the review and the degradation test. Dashed lines are hypotheses, including every people-side edge and the path to delivery metrics. The model, its changelog and the generator script are in the Legible site repository under model/, version 0.3, merged 29 October 2026.
