How we measure: counts with a remainder, never a percentage

Nobody knows how much an expert knows. A share of an unknown total has no denominator. The honest result is a count of items found, validated, written down and checked, with the remainder named.

System side: rebuild from the writing

An independent agent rebuilds the system from the captured material alone. The match against the original says how much the writing carries.

Person side: the successor’s questions

The questions asked of the expert in the last twelve months are put to the captured material. The expert grades the answers. After the handover, every question the successor asks is logged as answered, answered elsewhere, or lost.
